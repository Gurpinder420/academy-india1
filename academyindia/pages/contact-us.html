❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our academy, courses, and career support programs.

What courses does Digital Academy offer?

We offer a wide range of in-demand tech courses including Web Development, Data Science & AI, Digital Marketing, UI/UX Design, Cybersecurity, Cloud Computing, and Mobile App Development. All courses are taught by industry experts and are updated regularly to match market demands.

Do I need prior experience to enroll?

No prior experience is required for most of our beginner programs. We have courses tailored for complete beginners through to advanced professionals. Our team will assess your background and recommend the best starting point for your goals.

What is the duration of your courses?

Course durations vary from 3 months for focused skills courses to 12 months for comprehensive career programs. We offer both full-time (intensive) and part-time (flexible) schedules to accommodate working professionals and students.

How does your placement assistance work?

Our dedicated placement team maintains partnerships with 200+ companies. We provide resume reviews, mock interviews, LinkedIn optimization, and direct referrals. Our 95% placement rate reflects our commitment. Support continues for 6 months post-graduation.

Are online and offline classes available?

Yes! We offer both in-person classes at our campus and live online sessions. Our hybrid model lets you attend from anywhere while still benefiting from real-time interaction with mentors and peers. All sessions are recorded for later review.

What financing options are available?

We offer flexible payment plans, EMI options (0% interest for 12 months), scholarships for deserving students, and Income Share Agreements (ISA) where you pay only after getting placed. Early enrollment discounts are also available throughout the year.
